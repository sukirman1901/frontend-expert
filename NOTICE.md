# Authorship and research provenance

Frontend Expert—including its skill definitions, orchestration, routing, evaluation scenarios, and reference synthesis—is authored for this pack.

External standards, design systems, articles, galleries, and public skill repositories may be studied during research. Only the independently synthesized principles and rules are distributed in this pack. External skills are not bundled, vendored, or presented as part of Frontend Expert.

Researching a source does not transfer ownership of this pack to that source. Frontend Expert independently organizes its workflows, naming, decision boundaries, checklists, and quality gates.

If future work copies or modifies a substantial portion of third-party code or text, add the applicable copyright and license notice here before release.
